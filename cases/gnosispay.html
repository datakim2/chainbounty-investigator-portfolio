On 1 June 2026, an attacker drained dozens of GnosisPay Safes after a signature-verification flaw in the GnosisPay Delay module accepted attacker-crafted nested signature data. The crafted verification path reached attacker-prepared EIP-1271 contracts that returned the expected magic value, allowing queued transactions to transfer EURe and GNO from victim Safes.
